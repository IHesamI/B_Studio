PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED:
AUGUST 2026

1. OVERVIEW & GOOGLE PLAY COMPLIANCE

This Privacy Policy applies to the Boddy mobile game. We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining a secure and transparent gaming environment in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

We collect non-personal, anonymous gameplay analytics to help us balance levels, debug performance issues, and enhance player experience. The collected metrics include solely:

  • Gameplay outcomes (levels attempted, won, or defeated)
  • In-game customization selections (which cosmetic skins are equipped)
  • Anonymized session activity generated through a randomized timestamp ID

3. NO PERSONAL DATA COLLECTION

Boddy does NOT collect, request, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). We do not collect names, email addresses, device contacts, precise location data, phone numbers, or IP addresses. Player identity is tracked exclusively via a randomly generated ID created from timestamp data on your local device.

4. DATA SECURITY & SHARING

Because all collected analytics data is entirely aggregated and anonymous, it cannot be linked to your individual identity or device. We do not sell, rent, or trade your gameplay analytics data with third parties.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Boddy is designed for general audiences and complies with applicable global privacy standards. Since we do not collect personal data from any users, children's personal information is protected by design.

6. CONTACT US

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or analytics processing, please contact us via our developer support channel on GitHub or via our community Discord.
